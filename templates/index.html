Academic Project Overview

About the CampusAI Assistant

CampusAI is an enterprise-grade academic assistant created as a SYBBA (Computer Applications) Semester III capstone project. It bridges the communication gap between college administration and thousands of enrolled students by offering guaranteed 24/7 informational support.

✓
Dual Engine Resilience: Runs smoothly with Google Gemini AI or 100% offline using optimized FAQ matching.
✓
Admin Control Panel: Allows authorized college administrators to update fees, deadlines, and notices in real-time.
✓
No Misinformation: Fallback mechanisms ensure no fictitious dates or fees are ever presented to students.
